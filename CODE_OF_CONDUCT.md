# Code of conduct

Pance is a place to work on accessible, useful software. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome people of every background, experience level, language and ability.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive. No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Respect decisions. The maintainer has the final say on what is accepted (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)). Disagreement is welcome; repeated pressure is not.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use a private [security advisory](https://github.com/lermajonathan/pance-ds/security/advisories/new) marked "conduct", or contact the maintainer through the profile on GitHub.
